# Iron Key Challenge: The Five Researchers

## Problem Statement

Five researchers live in five different colored houses arranged in a row. Each researcher:
- Has a different nationality
- Works on a different AI model
- Drinks a different beverage
- Has a different pet
- Uses a different programming language

## Clues

1. The British researcher lives in the red house
2. The Swedish researcher has a dog as a pet
3. The Danish researcher drinks tea
4. The green house is immediately to the left of the white house
5. The researcher in the green house drinks coffee
6. The researcher who works on GPT has a bird
7. The researcher in the yellow house works on Claude
8. The researcher in the middle house drinks milk
9. The Norwegian researcher lives in the first house
10. The researcher who works on Gemini lives next to the one with a cat
11. The researcher with a horse lives next to the one who works on Claude
12. The researcher who works on LLaMA drinks orange juice
13. The Japanese researcher works on Mistral
14. The Norwegian researcher lives next to the blue house
15. The researcher who works on Gemini has a neighbor who drinks water

## Question

**Who has the fish as a pet?**

## Output Format

Write your answer to `/app/solutions/iron_answer.txt` as a single line:

```
<Nationality>
```

For example, if you think the British researcher has the fish, write:
```
British
```

Valid nationalities: British, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Japanese

**WARNING: Wrong answer = INSTANT DEATH!**
